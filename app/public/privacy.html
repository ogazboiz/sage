Sage — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 May 2026

Sage ("we", "the app") is a non-custodial wallet companion that lets a user issue an AI agent a per-task USDC spending cap on the Solana blockchain. This page explains what data the app handles.

What we collect

Sage does not run a backend account system. The app does not collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information to any server we operate. Specifically:

  • Wallet address. Public key only. Used to derive your vault PDA on Solana and sign on-chain instructions via Mobile Wallet Adapter. Never transmitted off-chain except when you explicitly sign an MWA prompt.
  • Agent keypair. A 32-byte ephemeral Ed25519 keypair generated on first vault initialisation. Stored in AsyncStorage on the device. Never leaves the device. Used to sign autonomous loop transactions on-chain.
  • Microphone audio (only when you tap "Start session" in Talk). Streamed live to ElevenLabs over WebRTC for the conversational AI agent. Not recorded or stored by Sage.

Third-party services

The app makes network requests to:

  • Solana RPC (api.devnet.solana.com or your configured endpoint) — to read vault state and submit signed transactions. Public read of public on-chain data.
  • LI.FI (li.quest, earn.li.fi) — to fetch cross-chain bridge quotes and yield data. No personal data sent.
  • ElevenLabs (api.elevenlabs.io) — for voice agent. Audio streams while the session is active; only when you start a session.
  • Briefing service (your configured endpoint) — pays per-call x402 micropayments from the vault. Receives the on-chain transaction signature for settlement; no personal data.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect analytics or telemetry.
  • We do not place tracking cookies or pixels.
  • We do not share data with advertising networks.
  • We do not custody your funds. All USDC is held in a Solana program-derived account (PDA) that only your wallet authorises.
